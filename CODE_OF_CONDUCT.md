# 📜 Code of Conduct — Cherry-Social

> Built by **Cherry Computer Ltd.**

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in **Cherry-Social** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Examples of positive behavior:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, trolling, or insulting comments
- Publishing others' private information
- Any form of discrimination

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers at the [Issues page](https://github.com/Infinite-Networker/Cherry-Social/issues).
